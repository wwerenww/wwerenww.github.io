1. Data we collect

The current version of the app does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or postal address in order to play the game.

The app may store limited gameplay data on your device, including level progress, token balance, current round state, and basic app preferences. This information is used only to provide the gameplay experience.

2. In-app purchases

The app may offer consumable token purchases through Apple’s App Store.

  • Payments are processed by Apple.
  • The app does not directly collect or store card details.
  • Transaction status information may be received from Apple to unlock purchased content.

3. Advertising and consent

The current release may show interstitial ads and rewarded ads. Third-party advertising partners may process limited technical data to deliver ads, measure performance, and manage consent.

  • Device identifiers
  • Ad interaction data
  • Diagnostic information
  • Approximate location derived from IP address, depending on the ad provider

Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only after the user makes a clear choice to watch one for an in-app benefit.

If tracking permission is required, the app will request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework before engaging in tracking.

4. Analytics and sharing

The current version of the app does not use a separate analytics service unless such a service is added later. If analytics or new advertising features are added, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated.

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared only when necessary to provide App Store purchases, advertising, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or other core app functionality supported by Apple or integrated service providers.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not knowingly designed to collect personal data from children.

6. Your choices and updates

You may stop using the app at any time. If future versions include account systems, cloud sync, or user-submitted content, additional access, correction, or deletion instructions will be published here.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same public URL with a revised date.
